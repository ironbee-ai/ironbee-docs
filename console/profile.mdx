Your profile shows who you are in IronBee. Open **Settings** from the sidebar or from your user menu, then choose **Profile** under **Personal**.

---

## Your details

<img className="ib-shot ib-shot-light" src="/images/console/settings/profile-light.png" alt="Profile card with the name, the email marked Verified by Google, Google OAuth as the authentication method, and the Show profile picture switch" />
<img className="ib-shot ib-shot-dark" src="/images/console/settings/profile-dark.png" alt="Profile card with the name, the email marked Verified by Google, Google OAuth as the authentication method, and the Show profile picture switch" />

| Field | What it shows |
|-------|---------------|
| Name | The card's heading: your name as your sign-in provider reports it, or your email if there's none |
| **Email** | Your email address, marked as verified by your sign-in provider |
| **Authentication** | How you sign in: **Google OAuth**, **GitHub OAuth** or **Email & Password** |

---

## Avatar

When your sign-in provider supplies a profile picture, the **Avatar** section offers a **Show profile picture** switch. It's off by default. Turn it on to show your picture in the user menu and on this page. The choice is saved in your browser.
