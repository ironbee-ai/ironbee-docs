title: Findings

---

**Findings** are observations that IronBee's [analysis](/console/analysis) identified in a project's sessions. Examples:
- a file with a high fix-cycle rate
- a pattern of retries
- a habit worth changing

Each finding is grounded in actual session data. Findings aren't necessarily bugs; they describe how the work is going.

Findings are tracked per project. Every daily analysis replaces the project's findings, and the list shows the active ones.

---

## Open a project's findings

Open **Findings** in the sidebar and pick a project. You can also click a findings count on a [project card](/console/projects#card-view), or follow a finding from a recommendation's causal chain.

## Severity

| Severity | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **Information** | A descriptive observation. No action is needed |
| **Concern** | Worth watching. It may have a linked recommendation |
| **Critical** | A serious degradation. It should have a linked recommendation |

Only **Concern** and **Critical** findings can have recommendations attached.

---

## Areas

| Area | What it covers |
|------|----------------|
| **Quality** | Verification thoroughness, fix effectiveness, recurring failures |
| **Efficiency** | Time spent on rework, slow sessions |
| **Patterns** | Files and issues that recur across sessions |
| **Behavior** | The agent's working habits |

---

## Counts

Four tiles at the top count the project's active findings: **Critical**, **Concern**, **Information** and **Total Findings**.

---

## Filter and search

- Filter by severity (**All severities**) and by area (**All areas**).
- **Search title…** matches finding titles from 3 characters.
- **Clear filters** resets them.

When you arrive from a link to one finding or one analysis, a **Finding** or **Analysis** chip pins the list to it. Remove the chip to see every finding.

---

## The findings table

| Column | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Severity** | Critical, Concern or Information |
| **Title** | The observation |
| **Area** | Quality, Efficiency, Patterns or Behavior |
| **Created** | When the finding was generated |

Every column sorts. The newest findings come first. Show 20, 50 or 100 rows per page.

---

## Finding details

Click a row to open the finding:

- The severity, the area, the project and when it was created.
- The title and the full description, with the evidence and metrics behind it. Some findings add a table or a chart.
- **View linked recommendation**, for **Concern** and **Critical** findings. It opens the recommendations that came from this finding.
