IronBee has three plans: **Free**, **Team** and **Enterprise**. Every plan ships with the full verification and intelligence core.

The plans and prices on this page mirror [ironbee.ai/pricing](https://ironbee.ai/pricing), the current source. If this page and the website ever differ, go by the website.

---

## Plans

The tables quote the website. In them, **Jobs** are verification runs, and a session is a single AI agent run that IronBee observes end to end, from the first change to the final verdict, including its timeline and telemetry.

| | Free | Team | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| For | Everything you need to start observing agent sessions with a small team. | Unlimited sessions and project-level analyses for your whole team. | Custom limits, security and support for large organizations. |
| Price | \$0 /month | \$15 /seat/month | Custom |
| Verification runs | 100 Jobs/mo | 100 Jobs/mo per seat | Unlimited Jobs |
| Sessions | 100 sessions/mo | Unlimited sessions | Unlimited sessions |
| Seats | 3 seats included | Seats as purchased | Custom pricing |
| Team management | No | Yes | Yes |
| Retention | 30-day retention | 90-day retention | Custom retention |

- Team costs \$15 per seat per month billed monthly, or \$12 per seat per month billed yearly (\$144 per seat a year). The **Annual** switch says **Save 20%**.
- On Team, you pay only for the seats you purchase. Every seat adds 100 verification runs a month.
- Free needs no credit card.
- You can upgrade, downgrade or cancel at any time. Moving back to Free keeps your account within the Free plan limits.

## Open the Pricing page

Owners and billing admins manage the plan. Open **Settings**, then **Workspace > Pricing**. Other roles don't see the page. See [Roles and permissions](/console/roles).

The header shows the account's name and its current plan. The page has two tabs: **Pricing Plans** and **History**.

---

## Pricing plans

The **Pricing Plans** tab shows:
- **Sessions this period**: your session usage. See [Session usage](#session-usage)
- the **Monthly** and **Annual** switch, which shows Team's price for each billing interval
- a card for each plan. **Current plan** marks yours. On Team, the Team card also shows your seats and billing interval, such as **15 seats · Monthly**

<img className="ib-shot ib-shot-dark" src="/images/console/settings/pricing-plans-dark.png" alt="Pricing Plans tab on the Free plan, with the Sessions this period meter, the Monthly and Annual switch, and the Free, Team and Enterprise cards" />

The buttons on the Free and Team cards depend on your plan:

| Your plan | Free card | Team card |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Free | **Your current plan** | **Upgrade to Team** |
| Team | **Downgrade to Free** | **Change seats**, **Manage billing**, **Cancel plan** |
| Team, with a cancellation scheduled | **Downgrade scheduled** | **Resume subscription**, **Manage billing** |
| Team, with a payment past due | **Downgrade to Free** | **Update payment method**, **Change seats**, **Cancel plan** |

On the Enterprise card, **Contact sales** opens IronBee's contact page, or you can email [support@ironbee.ai](mailto:support@ironbee.ai).

### Upgrade to Team

1. Pick **Monthly** or **Annual** above the cards, and click **Upgrade to Team**.
2. In the **Upgrade to Team** panel, check the **Billing interval**.
3. Under **Seats**, set the number of seats with the - and + buttons. The minimum shows below them, and it's never lower than the number of people in the account: members plus pending invitations. Owners also see those counts next to the seats.
4. Check the **Estimated total**. For **Annual**, it also shows the yearly amount.
5. Click **Continue to payment**, and pay on Stripe Checkout, which shows the final amount.

<img className="ib-shot ib-shot-light" src="/images/console/settings/pricing-upgrade-light.png" alt="Upgrade to Team panel with the Monthly and Annual billing intervals, the Seats stepper, the Estimated total, and the Cancel and Continue to payment buttons" />
<img className="ib-shot ib-shot-dark" src="/images/console/settings/pricing-upgrade-dark.png" alt="Upgrade to Team panel with the Monthly and Annual billing intervals, the Seats stepper, the Estimated total, and the Cancel and Continue to payment buttons" />

After you pay, you return to **Pricing Plans**:
- **Activating your Team plan** shows while the plan switches over. This usually takes a few seconds, and **Team plan is active** confirms it.
- If it takes longer, **Payment received** asks you to refresh shortly to see your Team plan.

If you leave the checkout without paying, your plan doesn't change.

### Change seats

Members and pending invitations each take a seat. When every seat is in use, **Invite team member** on the [Team](/console/team) page says so, and shows the owner a **View pricing** link.

To change the number of seats on Team:
1. Click **Change seats**.
2. In the **Change seats** dialog, set the new number. Prorated charges or credits appear on your next invoice.
3. Click **Update seats**.

You can't go below the number of people who use the account. For an owner, the dialog then lists who to remove first: pending invitations, then the newest members. Remove them on the [Team](/console/team) page and try again.

### Billing details and payments

**Manage billing** opens the Stripe billing portal.

If a payment fails, the Team card shows **Payment past due**, and its main button becomes **Update payment method**, which opens the same portal.

### Cancel or resume Team

1. Click **Cancel plan** on the Team card, or **Downgrade to Free** on the Free card.
2. **Cancel Team plan?** tells you:
   - the date the Team plan stays active until. After that, the account moves to Free, with the Free plan's limits
   - that unused time is not refunded
   - how many people will lose access, when the account has more people than Free's seats
3. Click **Cancel plan** to confirm, or **Keep plan** to close the dialog.

Until that date, the Team card shows **Cancels on** with the date, and the Free card shows **Downgrade scheduled**. To stay on Team, click **Resume subscription**.

### Session usage

**Sessions this period**, at the top of **Pricing Plans**, shows:
- the sessions used and your plan's limit, with how many remain. On Team, it shows the count and **Unlimited**
- the dates of the current period
- when the numbers were last updated

When you've used 80% of the limit, the meter turns to a warning. On Free, the warning suggests upgrading to Team. At the limit, it shows **Session limit reached**: new telemetry is paused until the period resets or you upgrade.

Until the first usage snapshot, the meter shows **Usage data is being collected**. The first snapshot appears within an hour.

### Banners across the console

A plan problem also shows as a banner at the top of every console page, one at a time:

| Banner | When | Button |
|--------|------|--------|
| **Session limit reached** | The account used its sessions for the period. New telemetry is rejected until the period resets | **Upgrade**, for owners and billing admins |
| **Payment past due** | IronBee couldn't charge your card | **Update payment method**, for owners and billing admins |
| **More people than seats** | More people use the account than its plan has seats | **Manage team** for owners and admins, **View pricing** for billing admins |

You can close the **More people than seats** banner. It comes back later while the account still has more people than seats.

## History

The **History** tab lists the account's invoices, one year at a time. Pick the year in the top-right corner.

<img className="ib-shot ib-shot-light" src="/images/console/settings/pricing-history-light.png" alt="History tab with a year picker and a table of invoices with their date, description, amount, Paid status and a Download PDF link" />
<img className="ib-shot ib-shot-dark" src="/images/console/settings/pricing-history-dark.png" alt="History tab with a year picker and a table of invoices with their date, description, amount, Paid status and a Download PDF link" />

Each invoice has:
- **Date**, **Description** and **Amount**
- **Status**: **Paid**, **Open**, **Failed** or **Void**
- **Invoice**: **Download PDF**, when a PDF is available

Click **Load more** for older invoices. Before your first payment, the tab shows **No payments yet**.
