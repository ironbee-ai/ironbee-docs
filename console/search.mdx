The console has two kinds of search:
- the **command palette**, for jumping to a project
- the **Search** page, for querying the data behind your projects

---

## Command palette

Press `⌘K` (or `Ctrl+K`), or click **Search projects or anything...** in the top bar.

- Type at least 3 characters to find a project by name. Picking one opens its [Verifications](/console/verifications) tab.
- With an empty query, the palette shows your **Recent** projects and **Navigation** shortcuts: **Go to Projects**, **Go to Analyses**, **Go to Findings** and **Go to Recommendations**.

## The Search page

Open **Search** in the sidebar to build a query over your account's data.

1. Under **Data Sources**, pick what to query: users, projects, sessions, verifications, fixes, findings, recommendations, pull requests or issues.
2. Choose **List** to see rows, or **Aggregate** to count and summarize.
3. Build the query:
   - In **List** mode, pick the **Columns** to show.
   - In **Aggregate** mode, add a **Group by** dimension and **Metrics** such as `COUNT(*)`.
   - Add **Filters** to narrow the rows in either mode.
4. Pick a time range. It starts at **Last 7 Days**.
5. Click **Run query**.

<img className="ib-shot ib-shot-light" src="/images/console/insights/search-query-builder-light.png" alt="The Search page with Verifications selected under Data Sources, an Aggregate query grouped by Project with a COUNT(*) metric, and its results as a table" />
<img className="ib-shot ib-shot-dark" src="/images/console/insights/search-query-builder-dark.png" alt="The Search page with Verifications selected under Data Sources, an Aggregate query grouped by Project with a COUNT(*) metric, and its results as a table" />

The **Results** show as a **Table**. An **Aggregate** query that groups by one dimension, by a **Time bucket**, or by both can also show as a **Chart**. Click **Export** to download the results as **CSV** or **PDF**.
