Owners and admins manage who has access to the account. Open **Settings**, then **Workspace > Team**. The page has two tabs: **Members** and **Invites**.

For what each role can do, see [Roles and permissions](/console/roles).

---

## Members

The **Members** tab lists everyone in the account:
- **User**: their name and email
- **Role**
- **Invited By**: who sent their invitation
- **Invited At** and **Accepted At**
- **Last Login**

<img className="ib-shot ib-shot-light" src="/images/console/settings/team-members-light.png" alt="Team page on the Members tab, with a search box and a table of members, their roles, invitation and login dates, and a Remove button" />
<img className="ib-shot ib-shot-dark" src="/images/console/settings/team-members-dark.png" alt="Team page on the Members tab, with a search box and a table of members, their roles, invitation and login dates, and a Remove button" />

Search by name or email. Click the **User**, **Invited At**, **Accepted At** or **Last Login** header to sort.

To change a member's role, pick a new one in the **Role** column and confirm with **Change role**. To remove someone, click **Remove** in the **Actions** column and confirm **Remove member?**. Nobody can change their own role, and the owner role can't be assigned.

---

## Invite a teammate

1. On the **Invites** tab, click **Invite member**.
2. In **Invite team member**, enter the **Email address** and pick a **Role**. **Member** is the default.
3. Click **Send invitation**.

<img className="ib-shot ib-shot-light" src="/images/console/settings/invite-member-dialog-light.png" alt="Invite team member dialog with an Email address field, the Role set to Member, and the Send invitation button" />
<img className="ib-shot ib-shot-dark" src="/images/console/settings/invite-member-dialog-dark.png" alt="Invite team member dialog with an Email address field, the Role set to Member, and the Send invitation button" />

The recipient has 7 days to accept. If the email can't be sent, the dialog shows the invitation link so you can share it yourself.

The **Invites** tab lists invitations that are pending or expired, with their **Email**, **Role**, **Status** and **Expires** date:
- **Revoke** a pending one, and confirm **Revoke invitation?**.
- **Resend** an expired one.

<img className="ib-shot ib-shot-light" src="/images/console/settings/team-invites-light.png" alt="Team page on the Invites tab, with the Invite member button, a Pending invitation with Revoke and an Expired invitation with Resend" />
<img className="ib-shot ib-shot-dark" src="/images/console/settings/team-invites-dark.png" alt="Team page on the Invites tab, with the Invite member button, a Pending invitation with Revoke and an Expired invitation with Resend" />

An accepted invitation leaves the list, and the person appears under **Members**.

---

## Accept an invitation

The person you invite gets an email with a link. The link opens a **You've been invited** page with the account, the role and the expiry date.

- Signed in: click **Accept invitation**.
- Not signed in: click **Continue with Google**, **Continue with GitHub** or **Sign in with email**, using the invited email address.

Once accepted, the console switches to the team's account. See [Sign in and team access](/console/sign-in).
