title: Evidence
description: Where the recording, screenshots and logs of each run are, on IronBee and on your runner.
---

Every run keeps the evidence behind its verdict. Where you find it depends on where the verification ran.

---

## Runs on IronBee

The evidence of a run on IronBee is in the IronBee Console:
- the recording
- screenshots
- the actions the agent took
- network requests, traces and logs

Open it from the **View session in IronBee Console** link in the [report](/github-action/guides/running-in-ci#the-report), from the `job_url` output, or from the project's **Verifications** tab. See [Verification detail](/console/verification-detail).

When the app ran on your runner, the action also uploads the `ironbee-application-log` artifact. It holds:
- the app's output
- the output of `app_logs_command`, when you set it

The artifact is kept for 30 days. Use it when a verification fails because the app itself stopped or errored.

---

## Runs on your runner

A run on your runner records to the IronBee Console and also uploads the `ironbee-verification-evidence` artifact, kept for 30 days:

```
.ironbee/artifacts/
  cycle-1/
    screenshots/
    recordings/
  cycle-2/
    screenshots/
    recordings/
  claude-output.log
  ironbee-devtools-mcp.log
  sessions/<id>/        # verdict.json, actions.jsonl
```

- Screenshots and recordings are grouped by verification cycle.
- `claude-output.log` holds the agent's output.
- `ironbee-devtools-mcp.log` is the DevTools server's log.

The report links to the artifact, and the `artifacts_url` output carries its download URL for later steps.

---

## Console links

The report's links point at `console.ironbee.ai`. To use a different host, set `ironbee_console_url` to the hostname, without a scheme:

```yaml
- uses: ironbee-ai/ironbee-action@v1.1.0
  with:
    ironbee_api_key: ${{ secrets.IRONBEE_API_KEY }}
    ironbee_console_url: 'console.example.com'
```
