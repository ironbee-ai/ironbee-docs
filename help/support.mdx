Need help with IronBee, or want to talk to the team? Here's how to reach us.

- Email: [support@ironbee.ai](mailto:support@ironbee.ai)
- Discord: [IronBee Community](https://discord.gg/xqBJy53dyJ)
- Slack: [IronBee Community](https://join.slack.com/t/ironbeeai-community/shared_invite/zt-446h3zo13-KU7U_E711F4mjLfw1mHeyw)
- X: [@ironbee_ai](https://x.com/ironbee_ai)
- LinkedIn: [ironbee-ai](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ironbee-ai)

You can also open **Help** in the console's sidebar for answers to common questions, keyboard shortcuts and links, or start a chat with us from the console after you sign in. See [ironbee.ai/support](https://ironbee.ai/support) for more.

---

## What to include

When you reach out, include:
- your account email and project name
- a link to the affected verification run or deployment, or the session ID for a CLI session
- what you expected, and what happened instead

That lets us investigate quickly.

---

## Response times

We reply within one business day. Business hours are Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 UTC+3. Issues that block a production deploy are prioritized.
