Every run keeps the evidence behind its verdict. Where you find it depends on where the verification ran.

---

## Runs on IronBee

The evidence of a run on IronBee is in the IronBee Console:
- the recording
- screenshots
- the actions the agent took
- network requests, traces and logs

Open it from the **View session in IronBee Console** link in the [report](/gitlab-component/guides/running-in-ci#the-report), from the `IRONBEE_JOB_URL` output, or from the project's **Verifications** tab. See [Verification detail](/console/verification-detail).

---

## The job's artifacts

The job uploads one artifact archive, `ironbee-verification-evidence`, whether the job passes or fails. GitLab keeps it for 30 days. Browse it from the job's page, under **Job artifacts**. It holds:

| File | When |
|------|------|
| `report.md` | Every run: the report, since GitLab has no job summary |
| `app.log` | When the component started your app: the app's output |
| `app-service-logs.txt` | When you set `app_logs_command`: the app's own logs |
| The verification evidence | Local mode, when the run produced any. See below |

Use the app logs when a verification fails because the app itself stopped or errored.

### Runs on your runner

A run on your runner records to the IronBee Console and also puts its evidence in the artifact:

```
cycle-1/
  screenshots/
  recordings/
cycle-2/
  screenshots/
  recordings/
claude-output.log
ironbee-devtools-mcp.log
sessions/<id>/        # verdict.json, actions.jsonl
```

- Screenshots and recordings are grouped by verification cycle.
- `claude-output.log` holds the agent's output.
- `ironbee-devtools-mcp.log` is the DevTools server's log.

The report links to the artifacts, and the `IRONBEE_ARTIFACTS_URL` output carries the link for later jobs.

---

## Console links

The report's links point at `console.ironbee.ai`. To use a different host, set `ironbee_console_url` to the hostname, without a scheme:

```yaml
include:
  - component: gitlab.com/ironbee-ai/ironbee-gitlab/verify@v1
    inputs:
      ironbee_console_url: 'console.example.com'
```
